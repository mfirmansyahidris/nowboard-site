Nowboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Contact: manca.fi@gmail.com

Application: fi.dev.nowboard (Android)

No account, no ads

Nowboard needs no account and shows no advertising. The advertising identifier is not collected.

Stored on your device

Your place, widget settings, language, to-do tasks and cached weather, rates and prayer times stay on your device. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes them.

Location

You choose how Nowboard finds your place:

(1) device location, only after you grant approximate location. Coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and sent to BigDataCloud (place name) and Open-Meteo (time zone and weather);

(2) network detection, which asks GeoJS to estimate your city from your internet address;

(3) a typed search, sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Location is never read in the background and is not sent to Nowboard.

Weather and rates

Weather forecasts come from Open-Meteo for your chosen place. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter (European Central Bank reference rates). These services receive the request and your connection information only.

Screen time

If you allow usage access in Android settings, Nowboard reads today's app usage on your device to show it in widgets. It is never uploaded.

Optional diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase are off by default. Only users who confirm they are 18 or older can turn on "Share crash reports and usage statistics"; the age answer is not stored or sent. When on, they process crash logs, app interactions, device and app details and installation identifiers to fix problems and measure usage. Turning it off stops further collection. Your place, tasks and screen time are never included.

Children

Nowboard is suitable for all ages. Children and teenagers cannot enable diagnostics, so no diagnostic data is collected from them. Nowboard does not ask children for contact details.

Changes

Updates to this policy will carry a new date.
